Sanitation Revolving Fund Simulator

Optimize Impact, Sustainability, and Reach.

💡 Introduction (LDC Context)

This simulator models a Revolving Fund for sanitation in Least Developed Countries (LDCs). We've pre-loaded a scenario for Malawi:

  • Capital: $4M Loan + $1M Grant
  • Goal: Test delivery toward the rural basic-sanitation need.
  • Challenge: Balance borrower pricing and portfolio revenue with fund solvency.

📚 How to Run

  1. 1. Auto-Load: The model loads the latest available dated World Bank observations and, where available, WHO health evidence for Malawi.
  2. 2. Run Model: Click "Recalculate Model" to run the scenario.
  3. 3. Calibrate: Rates and terms change portfolio cashflow; enterprise capacity and rollout assumptions change delivery. Grant-Funded Pacing changes which ledger funds production, not the total grant capital available.

🔄 Theory of Change (Revised)

This model uses a reconciled two-ledger monthly simulation. It runs integrity checks on every simulation to ensure cash continuity, unit-cost validity, and logical consistency.

If the fund forecasts a cash shortfall (based on your chosen Term), it will automatically slow down lending or hibernate to preserve liquidity.

Your task: Test whether the proposed programme can combine credible rollout, adequate liquidity, and investor repayment under documented assumptions. This is a scenario model, not a guarantee.

🚀 How to Use

  • Step 1: Select a country (loads dated country observations).
  • Step 2: Adjust the "Levers" (Interest Rates, Loan Terms, Grant %).
  • Step 3: Watch the Charts & Scorecard. Avoid "Insolvency"!
